German Cases · 四格讲解

The four cases (Kasus) show a noun's role in a sentence. English mostly uses word order; German uses endings on articles. · 英语讲解，中文关键词注释

Nominativ 主格

What it is: The "default" form of a noun — the subject doing the action, or the complement after sein/bleiben/werden.

When to use: ① Subject of the sentence (who/what does the verb). ② After sein (to be), werden (to become), bleiben (to stay) — these verbs don't change the case.
中文：主格是名词的"原始形态"，做主语或系动词后的表语。
GenderArticleExample noun
Masculine 阳derder Mann
Feminine 阴diedie Frau
Neuter 中dasdas Kind
Plural 复diedie Leute

🔊 Der Mann kommt aus Deutschland.

The man comes from Germany. (Der Mann = subject, doing the coming)

🔊 Sie ist eine Lehrerin.

She is a teacher. (eine Lehrerin = complement after "ist", stays Nominativ)

Akkusativ 宾格

What it is: The direct object — the thing/person receiving the action of most verbs.

When to use: ① Direct object of most verbs (haben, suchen, finden, nehmen, sehen, essen…). ② After prepositions durch, für, gegen, ohne, um.
中文：宾格是动作的直接承受者。关键：只有阳性单数冠词变（der→den），阴性/中性/复数不变！这是初学者最大的福音。
GenderNom→ AkkChanged?
Masculine 阳derdenYES ⚠️
Feminine 阴diedieno
Neuter 中dasdasno
Plural 复diedieno

🔊 Ich suche den Bahnhof.

I am looking for the train station. (den Bahnhof = direct object of "suche"; der→den because Bahnhof is masculine)

🔊 Ich habe ein Ticket.

I have a ticket. (ein Ticket = direct object; "ein" unchanged because Ticket is neuter)

Dativ 与格

What it is: The indirect object — the recipient/beneficiary of an action. Answers "to/for whom?"

When to use: ① Indirect object (Ich gebe dem Mann das Buch = I give the man the book). ② After prepositions aus, bei, mit, nach, seit, von, zu. ③ After some verbs (helfen, danken, gehören, antworten) that always take Dativ.
中文：与格表示动作的间接承受者（给谁、为谁）。记忆：阳单 der→dem，阴单 die→der（注意 der 跑到阴单去了，容易混！）。
GenderNom→ Dat
Masculine 阳derdem
Feminine 阴dieder
Neuter 中dasdem
Plural 复dieden + noun-n

🔊 Ich komme aus Deutschland.

I come from Germany. (aus + Dativ; Deutschland has no article, so no visible change)

🔊 Ich wohne in Berlin.

I live in Berlin. (in + Dativ for "where"; Berlin has no article)

Preposition "in" is tricky: in + Dat = where (location, "Ich wohne in Berlin"); in + Akk = where to (motion, "Ich gehe in die Stadt"). You'll learn this contrast in Day 31+.

Genitiv 属格

What it is: Possession — "of" something. Shows ownership or belonging.

When to use: ① Possession (das Auto des Mannes = the car of the man / the man's car). ② After prepositions während, wegen, statt, trotz. Note: In everyday spoken German, Genitiv is often replaced by "von + Dativ" (das Auto von dem Mann), but it's still used in writing and formal contexts.
中文：属格表"的/属于"。口语常被"von+与格"替代，但书面语和正式场合仍用属格。A1 阶段认得即可，不急着主动用。
GenderNom→ Gen
Masculine 阳derdes + noun-s
Feminine 阴dieder
Neuter 中dasdes + noun-s
Plural 复dieder + noun

🔊 Das ist das Auto des Mannes.

That is the car of the man. (des Mannes = Genitiv, masculine singular; noun adds -s)

🔊 Während der Woche arbeite ich.

During the week I work. (während + Genitiv; der Woche = Genitiv feminine, looks like Nominativ masculine — context tells you which)

Quick summary · 四格速记

Who does it? → Nominativ (subject)

What receives it? → Akkusativ (direct object) — only masculine changes: der→den

To/for whom? → Dativ (indirect object) — der→dem, die→der

Of what? → Genitiv (possession) — der→des, often + noun -s

A1 阶段重点掌握 Nominativ 与 Akkusativ；Dativ 认得常用介词搭配（aus/in/von/mit）；Genitiv 认得即可。Day 31+ 系统学冠词四格。
